## The Honest Truth About What Makes a Proposal Impressive

✅ **IMPRESSIVE:**
1. Opens with insight ("We already have the network, but it's not searchable")
2. Addresses the obvious objection ("Why not Google?")
3. Establishes competitive moat clearly
4. Specific details (tech stack, pricing, metrics)
5. Shows maturity (risk assessment, phased approach, realistic targets)
6. Confident, insider tone
7. Acknowledges what you're NOT doing
8. Professional presentation (formatting, white space, hierarchy)
9. Concrete numbers (market size, existing assets)
10. Clear next steps with ownership

❌ **NOT IMPRESSIVE:**
1. Generic positioning ("Build a community platform")
2. Ignoring obvious objections
3. No competitive analysis
4. Vague language ("professional," "comprehensive")
5. Ignoring risks
6. Overly cautious tone
7. Treating every feature as equally important
8. Poor formatting or hierarchy
9. No market sizing
10. Unclear accountability for next steps
