
FiTEN - Edison Product Launch 2026
What is FeTNA?

FeTNA is a Non Profit organization founded in 1988 with a mission to connect Global Tamils and to foster Tamil Entrepreneurship. FeTNA has been connecting Global Tamil Entrepreneurs for the last 21 years. 



What is FiTEN?

FiTEN is a flagship annual event hosted by FeTNA, is one of the largest events focused on entrepreneurship that attracts thousands of entrepreneurs, investors, corporate executives and visionaries.



Why attend FiTEN Edison?

The FeTNA International Tamil Entrepreneur Network (FiTEN) invites you to a global gathering of visionaries, innovators, and changemakers. On July 2, 2026, we’ll explore groundbreaking ideas and forge partnerships in the vibrant city of Edison, New Jersey. Be part of the revolution!



What is a Product Launch and What Are Its Benefits?  

The Product Launch is a new initiative by FiTEN designed to showcase innovative products on a prominent stage, featuring categories such as AI, Healthcare, Science, and Social Impact.  

A rare opportunity to launch your product in front of entrepreneurs, investors, technology specialist and great networking opportunity.
