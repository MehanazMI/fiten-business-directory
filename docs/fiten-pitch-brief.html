02  ·  Problem & Solution
The Gap We're Closing
FiTEN has spent 40 years building one of the most connected Tamil entrepreneur networks — but that network lives in event halls, not online. When the conference ends, connections disappear.
The Problem
🔍
No Discoverability
A Tamil founder in New Jersey has no way to find a Tamil software agency in Chennai. The platform simply doesn't exist — Tamil businesses are invisible to each other across borders.
🌐
No Community Context
Google, LinkedIn, and Yelp carry no Tamil identity or cultural trust. For high-stakes B2B decisions — legal, financial, technology — community credibility is everything.
📡
No B2B Signaling
There is no way to broadcast "I am looking for a partner, investor, or client" within the Tamil network. Business intent stays invisible and opportunities are missed.
The Solution
⚡
List in Minutes, Visible Globally
Any Tamil business submits once and appears in real time — searchable across cities, sectors, and continents, with no waiting.
🤝
Active B2B Signals
Businesses flag exactly what they need right now — Partner · Client · Investment · Hiring. Real intent, not just a static profile.
🏅
Community Trust Layer
FeTNA Sponsors, Women-Led businesses, Conference Speakers — curated identities backed by 40 years of FeTNA credibility that no algorithm can replicate.
🌐
Bilingual — English + Tamil
Full English and Tamil-script interface, built for the diaspora — not just translated, but designed for how Tamil professionals actually work.
